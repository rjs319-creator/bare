# external-evidence — what the published/practitioner record says about LLM-agent trading alpha (2026-09-09)

Question: is there credible evidence that multi-agent LLM systems produce LIVE, out-of-sample, cost-net alpha — as opposed to backtests? Sources fetched today; numbers are as reported by the papers/articles, not re-derived.

## 1. Headline

- **Zero systems in the literature demonstrate live, prospective, cost-net alpha in equities.** The only real-money public test (Alpha Arena S1) was a 17-day crypto-perps contest with six models: one +22%, one +4%, four large losses incl. GPT-5 −60%+. That is a coin-flip dispersion, not evidence.
- **The flagship backtests do not model costs and do not disclose training cutoffs** (TradingAgents), or **do not address the LLM knowing the test period** (AlphaAgent 2021-2025 test). Two 2025-26 review papers and three contamination papers say reported returns in this field are "largely unreliable" and can "reverse sign" under proper evaluation.
- **The one robust, replicated positive result — LLM news-headline sentiment (Lopez-Lira & Tang, JFE 2026)** — is (i) post-cutoff, (ii) real, (iii) concentrated in small caps and negative news, (iv) dead at 20 bps round-trip with ~190%/day turnover, and (v) decaying: Sharpe 6.54 (2021Q4) → 3.68 (2022) → 2.33 (2023) → 1.22 (Jan-May 2024). Institutions with direct market access can harvest it; a solo trader paying this app's cost-v3 (16 bps liquid / 60 bps small / 150 bps micro round trip) cannot.

## 3. By role — what survives

(a) **Agents as pickers.** No credible evidence. Post-cutoff benchmarks (StockBench) say most models lose to buy-and-hold; the only real-money test had a losing median; every strong backtest is either pre-cutoff, cost-free, or both. This app's own AI-screener ledgers (readthrough / anomaly / secondwave / crossasset / toneshift, all prospective, cost-net) agree: none is CI-positive at its contract, CrossAsset:Lead is sector beta.

(b) **Agents as factor/hypothesis generators feeding a quant harness.** The most defensible claim in the literature (AlphaAgent +8.7%/yr after fees, IR ~1) — but on an unaddressed-leakage window with undisclosed selection breadth; the ICLR-2026 benchmark exists because the community does not trust these numbers yet. Mechanically this is what this app's `research/experiments` registry already does with strict pre-registration, holdouts and BH-FDR — and it has produced 0 confirmed / 24 no-edge over 55 experiments. Agents make hypothesis generation cheaper; they do not change the base rate of true hypotheses, and every extra hypothesis raises the multiple-testing bar.

(c) **Agents as unstructured-text readers.** The only replicated positive LLM effect in finance lives here (headline sentiment → next-day drift), and it is real but (i) small-cap/negative-news concentrated, (ii) turnover-heavy and dead at 20 bps, (iii) visibly decaying with adoption. Disclosure-*change* signals (From Text to Alpha; 8-K/424B5 event extraction) are the more promising and less crowded direction, and this app's strongest retrospective result — the 424B5 dilution AVOID (−130 bps/5d, q 0.0002, 4/4 blocks, from EDGAR, no LLM needed) — sits exactly there. Note: When Valid Signals Fail shows IC>0.15 text features still lose to price-only across regime breaks.

(d) **Agents as risk/avoid filters.** Nothing published tests this directly, but it is the direction every negative finding points: the Lopez-Lira drift is strongest on *negative* news; the dilution flag is an AVOID; StockBench's better models won on drawdown control, not returns; ai-hedge-fund's design keeps the LLM out of the trade and inside the risk gate. The bar is also lower — an AVOID filter only has to identify a lane with CI<0, which this app's ledger already does with 25 FDR-robust negatives.

## 4. Contamination and small-sample rules (blunt)

1. Any LLM backtest whose window overlaps the model's training data is uninterpretable — mega-caps and index-level series are memorized verbatim (Oracle; LAP). TradingAgents (AAPL/GOOGL/AMZN, cutoffs undisclosed) fails this outright.
2. A 3-5 month window on 3-6 tickers at Sharpe 5-8 is luck or leakage. This app's own bar (≥50 resolved, ≥20 independent dates, date-clustered CI, 4 chronological blocks, BH-FDR) is stricter than anything in the literature reviewed.
3. Multi-agent "debate" adds cost, latency and a coordination breakeven spread; no paper shows the coordination itself earns net of that spread.
4. Costs: the one real signal dies at 20 bps. This app's cost-v3 small/micro tiers are 60/150 bps. Any headline-sentiment or social-attention strategy is dead on arrival in the universe this app screens.
5. Decay: the published Sharpe fell ~80% in 2.5 years as adoption rose. A solo developer arriving in 2026 with public Yahoo/Finnhub/FMP data and frontier models everyone else has is late to a crowded, shrinking trade.

## 5. What the evidence supports for THIS app (Yahoo/Finnhub/FMP Premium, EDGAR, StockTwits)

- **Do not build an agent picker.** No external evidence, and the app's own prospective AI-screener ledgers are already the experiment — negative.
- **Agents as a research factory only under the existing governance** (pre-registration in `research/experiments/registry.json`, post-cutoff-only evaluation, BH-FDR, holdout never re-run). Expect the same base rate the registry has shown; the value is throughput on falsification, not alpha per se.
- **The one text lane with external support is disclosure/event extraction from EDGAR** (8-K items, 424B5/S-3 dilution, guidance changes), evaluated as event studies vs sector at 5-20d, long-only AVOID first. It needs no LLM for the structured parts (the dilution result already exists) and an LLM only for unstructured item text; it must be judged strictly post-cutoff and cost-net at cost-v3 tiers.
- **Agents as an AVOID/risk layer is the only role with a plausibly positive expected value now** — it is where the literature's real effects concentrate (negative news, drawdown control) and where this app already has 25 FDR-robust negatives to act on.
- **Headline sentiment / StockTwits attention: dead for this user** — costs and decay; the app's own Attention lane is `inverted` (IC −0.154, t −3.0), i.e. the loudest names lose.
